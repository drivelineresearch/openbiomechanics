# Security Policy

## Reporting privately

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, exposed credential, private athlete information, or other sensitive content. Email **kyle@drivelinebaseball.com** with:

- the affected file, release asset, URL, or commit;
- steps to reproduce or locate the issue;
- the likely impact and who could be affected;
- any suggested mitigation; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not attach private datasets, credentials, or unnecessary personal information. A minimal redacted proof is preferred.

## Scope

Security reports may concern repository code, download/release integrity, dependencies, GitHub-hosted project surfaces, or accidental disclosure in public data and media. General scientific questions, documentation corrections, and ordinary bugs can use the normal contribution process once GitHub Issues are enabled.

The supported source version is the current `main` branch. Dataset/media release tags are immutable snapshots; a confirmed issue in an older release will be documented and, when necessary, corrected through a new release rather than silently replacing evidence.

## Response and disclosure

Maintainers will acknowledge and triage reports on a best-effort basis. Please allow time to investigate before public disclosure, especially when a report may involve human-subjects data or a third-party dependency. We will coordinate a fix, release note, or public advisory when appropriate and credit reporters who request attribution.
